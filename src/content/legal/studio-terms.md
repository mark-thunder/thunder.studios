These terms apply to every session at Thunder Studios. "You" means the person who books the session, and these terms also cover everyone you bring.

## Booking and payment

A session is booked once we confirm it in writing. Your booking confirmation or contract sets the room, the date and time, the price, what we provide, and when payment is due. A membership has its own agreement, which sets its price, what it includes and how to end it.

## Cancelling and rescheduling

The cancellation and rescheduling terms in your booking confirmation or contract apply. If we have to cancel your session, we offer you another time or a full refund of what you paid for it.

## Your session

Your session starts and ends at the times booked, including setup and packing up. Time past your booking is charged as your booking confirmation sets out, and only if the room is free.

## Who can come

You may bring the guests your session needs. Everyone follows these terms and our crew's directions. Anyone under 18 must be with a parent or guardian for the whole session.

## Care of the studio and equipment

Our crew runs the cameras, the microphones and the lights. Please ask before you move or adjust any studio equipment, and leave the room as you found it.

Tell us straight away about any damage. If you or your guests damage the studio or its equipment, you pay the reasonable cost to repair or replace it.

No smoking or vaping in the studio. Food and drink only where our crew allows.

## Your recordings and photos

Your booking confirmation or contract sets out what we deliver and when, and who owns the finished files. Unless it says otherwise, you own the content you record at the studio. We do not publish your recordings or photos without your written permission.

## Consent of the people you record

Texas law allows you to record a conversation you take part in, or one where a person in it agreed beforehand, unless the recording is for an unlawful purpose ([Texas Penal Code § 16.02(c)(4)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PE/htm/PE.16.htm#16.02)). Our rule is stricter: tell everyone on mic or on camera that they are being recorded, and get their permission before you publish what you record of them.

You are responsible for releases from your guests and for having the rights to any music, footage or other material you bring or use.

## What you may not record

Nothing illegal, nothing that infringes someone else's rights, and no sexually explicit content. We may stop a session that breaks these terms or puts people or equipment at risk.

## Your belongings

You are responsible for the belongings and equipment you bring. We are not liable for their loss or damage unless we caused it.

## Safety

Follow our crew's safety directions, keep exits and walkways clear, and tell us at once about any injury.

## Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability for a session is limited to what you paid for it, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses. Nothing in these terms limits a liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

## Events outside our control

We are not responsible for a session we cannot hold because of events outside our control, such as severe weather, a power cut or an emergency. We will offer you another time or a refund of what you paid for that session.

## Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

## Changes

When these terms change, we update the date at the top of this page. The version in force on the day you book applies to that booking.
