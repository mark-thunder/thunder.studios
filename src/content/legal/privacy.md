This policy covers the Thunder Studios website. It explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We do not sell your personal information.

Thunder Studios is a recording and photography studio in White Oak, Texas. This site is meant for visitors in the United States.

## What we collect

**When you send the contact form**, we receive what you type:

- name and email address (required)
- company name (optional)
- phone number (optional)
- the service you need, when you need it, and your budget range (required)
- your message (optional)
- your answer to one follow-up question, if the form asks one (optional)
- whether you want text messages about your enquiry (optional, unticked until you tick it)

**When you visit the site**, some details are recorded automatically:

- your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you request, in our hosting provider's server logs
- signals from your browser that our spam check reads to tell a person from a bot (see Cloudflare below)

The site runs no analytics, advertising or chat tools today. If we add any, we will list them here and in the Cookie Policy before they run.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, sensitive information such as health, financial account or government ID details.

## Why we use it

- to reply to your enquiry and to book and run your studio session
- to keep spam and automated submissions out of our inbox
- to keep business records and meet legal obligations

We do not use your information for decisions that have legal or similar effects on you, and we do not build advertising profiles.

## Who helps us handle it

We share information only with the companies that run parts of this site and our business for us. Each one receives only what its job needs.

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and runs Turnstile, the check on the contact form that tells people from bots. Turnstile reads browser signals and your IP address, sets no cookies, and discards the signals after the check.
- **TypeSafe AI** reads the text of your form submission to judge whether it is spam. A submission it rates as spam is not forwarded to us. When you finish typing in the project box, it also reads what you have entered so far to pick one follow-up question; we do not store that text until you send the form.
- **HighLevel (LeadConnector)** receives enquiries that pass both checks and holds our customer records.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

## Text messages

We text you only if you tick the box on the form. We text about your enquiry and your bookings, and message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your mobile number or your consent with anyone for their own marketing.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and client records for as long as we need them to work with you and to keep accurate business records, then delete them. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at the address below and we will reply within 45 days. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If you receive email from us, every message includes our postal address and a way to stop receiving more.

This site does not change how it works when a browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

## Children

This site is for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Access to enquiries is limited to the people who need it to do their work.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
