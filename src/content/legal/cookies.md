A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Some sites also use your browser's local and session storage, which work the same way. This page lists everything thunder.media stores, checked on the live site on the date above.

## Needed for the site to work

**Cloudflare Turnstile** runs on the contact form to tell people from bots. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

## Used for visit tracking and chat

These come from HighLevel (LeadConnector), our customer-relationship tool. They are not needed for the site to work.

- **Cookie `lc_session_tk_…`** links the pages you view during one visit, so an enquiry can be matched to how you found us.
- **Local storage `v2_contact_session_…`, `v2_session_history_…`, `v2_history_…`, `v3_first_session_event_…`** record your visit history and first visit on this site for the same purpose.
- **Session storage `i18n.en-us`** holds the chat window's language setting and is cleared when you close the tab.

## Used for site statistics

These come from **Google Analytics**. They count visits and enquiries so we can see what brings people to the site. They are not needed for the site to work.

- **Cookie `_ga`** tells one browser's visits apart. It lasts up to 2 years.
- **Cookie `_ga_VC5VZY83DV`** keeps track of the current visit. It lasts up to 2 years.

## Embedded videos

Some pages play videos from **YouTube** in its [privacy-enhanced mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780) (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may store data in your browser, mainly once you press play, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not use advertising cookies. Apart from Google Analytics and YouTube, as above, we do not let other companies place cookies in your browser.

## Your consent

This site is meant for visitors in the United States, where these tools do not need a consent banner. Using the site means you accept them. You can turn them off at any time, as below.

## Turning them off

You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser's settings. The site keeps working if you do. The chat window may stop working, we will not be able to link your visit to an enquiry, and your visit will not show in our statistics.

## Changes

If we add or remove anything on this list, we update this page and the date at the top.
