A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. Some sites also use your browser's local and session storage, which work the same way. This page lists everything the Thunder Studios website stores.

## What the site stores

The site sets no cookies.

- **Local storage `theme`** remembers whether you chose the light or dark look in the menu, so the next page opens the same way. It is saved only if you use that switch, and it stays in your browser until you clear it. It is not sent to us.

**Cloudflare Turnstile** runs on the contact form to tell people from bots. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

We do not use analytics, advertising or chat tools, and we do not let other companies place cookies in your browser.

## Turning it off

You can delete site storage in your browser's settings. The site keeps working; it opens in the light look until you choose again.

## Changes

If we add or remove anything on this list, we update this page and the date at the top before it runs.
